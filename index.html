Astrology from numerology

Numerology assigns each root number to a planet. Your psychic number (birth day) shows how you see yourself, your destiny number (full birth date) shows what life brings. Your sun sign has its own ruling planet, so the two can agree or pull apart.

Systems of numerology

Pythagorean

Letters run A=1 to I=9, then restart, so J=1 and S=1. The most common Western method. Used for Expression, Soul Urge and Personality here.

Unit (Hebraic) system

Used by Johari and Sepharial. A, I, J, Q, Y = 1; B, C, K, R = 2; G, L, S = 3; D, M, T = 4; E, N = 5; U, V, W, X = 6; O, Z = 7; F, H, P = 8. Nothing is 9. Name numbers on this site use it by default.

Johari: psychic, destiny and name

Three numbers describe a person. The psychic number is the birth day reduced to one digit (how you see yourself, strongest until about 35 to 40). The destiny number is the sum of the whole birth date (your fruits of past actions, stronger after 35). The name number comes from the full name in the Unit system. Compound birth days, exalted numbers, zeros in the date and year numbers are all read from the book.

Sepharial: the Kabala of Numbers

Sepharial (1920) reads numbers as symbols, with the Hebraic alphabet for scripture and tarot, and a phonetic alphabet tied to the planets. He lists planet numbers (Sun 1, Moon 7 or 2, Jupiter 3, Mercury 5, Venus 6, Saturn 8, Mars 9, Sun also 4) and gives a meaning for each name total up to 50.
